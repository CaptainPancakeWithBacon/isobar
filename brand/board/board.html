What we heard

The brief

An interface layer for GNOME 50 that takes its colours from your wallpaper, at all times: a slim strip, a big clock, cards that go light or dark with the picture. The identity must work on any wallpaper, so the mark has no colour of its own.

calmquietsoftadaptivepersonal

Success: recognisable at 16 px as an extension and tab icon, works in one colour (it will take the wallpaper's), clearly not the old contour-map or island identity.

Final design

Dusk

A solid half disc whose other half dissolves into contour lines: light and dark in one shape, the lines of an isobar map.

  • Light and dark in one silhouette, which is exactly what the interface does when the wallpaper changes
  • Contour lines keep the Isobar name and the old project's idea, without the island pill
  • Reads at 16 px as a half disc with arcs; three cuts (master, small, favicon) are drawn for each size band
  • Has no colour of its own: the mark takes the wallpaper's hue
